Your screen time, converted into books

How many books your daily screen time adds up to per month and per year, and how many 20 minutes a day would get you through. The calculation runs in your browser: nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Screen time per day

iPhone: Settings › Screen Time › See All App & Website Activity. Android: Settings › Digital Wellbeing. Starting value: 3 h 21 min, the average leisure screen time reported in France (CNL survey 2025, p. 45). Replace it with yours.

How the number is calculated

Books per year = minutes per day × words read per minute × 365 ÷ words in the book; per month, divide by 12. With the starting values: 201 minutes × 238 words × 365 days = 17.5 million words, or 273 copies of The Awakening (64,028 words). At 20 minutes a day, the same formula gives 27 books of that length in a year.

The reading rate comes from Marc Brysbaert’s meta-analysis “How many words do we read per minute?” (Journal of Memory and Language, 2019): 190 studies, 18,573 participants. He puts adult silent reading in English at 238 words a minute for non-fiction and 260 for fiction. Most of those measurements used short texts read in a lab; a real reading session, with pauses and re-reading, goes a little slower. Lower the rate if you want a cautious estimate.

What the result does not say

The big number assumes you would read for all your screen time, which nobody does: phones are also for calls, maps and payments. The useful line is the 20-minute one, the daily target of Vread’s program. At that pace, The Great Gatsby takes 11 days.
