Privacy and terms of the 30-day program list

Version of November 3, 2026 (consent text p30-en-2026-11-03). This page covers only the waiting list of the 30-day reading program, signed up for on the English page. It does not cover the Vread app, which has its own policy at vread.fr/legal/privacy.

Who is responsible

READ FRANCE SAS, 11 rue Rouvet, 75019 Paris, France, SIREN 988 741 773, publisher of Vread. Contact: octave@vread.fr. This inbox is read by the READ FRANCE team and by Octave, its AI assistant, which passes any request about your data on to the founders.

What is recorded when you sign up

  • your email address and, if you give it, your first name;
  • the language of the page (English) and the price it showed ($74.99 a year);
  • the date and time of sign-up, the sign-up page and the version of the consent text;
  • the campaign parameters in the page address (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_term, utm_content) and the domain name of the site you came from.

Nothing else: no IP address, no password, no payment details. The list is not linked to the Vread app, to a Vread account or to any other file.

Why

To email you on the day the program opens, planned for January 2027, and to count how many people sign up. You will not get a newsletter or offers for another product. Your address is not sold, rented or shared.

Legal basis: your consent (Article 6(1)(a) of the EU General Data Protection Regulation), given by ticking the box on the form. You can withdraw it at any time without giving a reason.

How long

Until the opening email, then three months. If the program does not open, the list is deleted by June 30, 2027 at the latest.

Where the data is kept

The list is a Google Sheets spreadsheet belonging to READ FRANCE, filled in by a Google Apps Script. The pages are hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Both companies are based in the United States and take part in the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, which governs transfers of personal data out of the European Union.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, its correction, deletion or portability, and object to its use, by writing to octave@vread.fr from the address you signed up with. We answer within one month. If you are not satisfied with the answer, you can complain to the CNIL, the French data protection authority (cnil.fr/en/complaints), or to the authority where you live.

Audience measurement, without cookies

These pages count, anonymously and in aggregate, page views, how many visitors reach the price, clicks on the sign-up button, form openings and submissions, along with the referring site’s domain, utm parameters, scroll depth, sections reached, questions opened, the name of the last field touched (never its content), technical errors and loading speed. Only daily totals are kept, in the same spreadsheet. No cookie, no identifier, no IP address. Nothing is measured if your browser sends “Do Not Track” or “Global Privacy Control”. This follows the French rules for audience measurement exempt from consent (Article 82 of the French Data Protection Act, CNIL recommendation of September 17, 2020).

What these pages keep in your browser

For the life of the tab (sessionStorage): the utm parameters you arrived with. They do not identify you. You can clear them by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

Terms of the list

Joining the list is free and commits you to nothing. No payment is taken on these pages and no pre-order is offered. The price on the page, $74.99 a year in US dollars with a 14-day free trial, is the price announced for the opening; local taxes may apply. The opening email will give the price and the terms of sale, and you decide then. You can leave the list at any time by writing to octave@vread.fr.
