In two years, people in France lost 10 minutes of reading a day

In January 2025, people in France aged 15 and over spent an average of 31 minutes a day reading books in their free time, down from 41 minutes in 2023. Over the same free time, they spent 3 hours 21 minutes a day looking at a screen.1

Willpower is only part of the story. Your phone is designed to keep you on it.

How it works

  1. Read wherever you like: paper, e-reader, it doesn’t matter.Audiobooks count too. Vread doesn’t replace your book; the app follows your reading.
  2. Every 30 to 40 pages, one question shows you read and understood.The answer is a single word.

Why it works

Blocking apps is half the job. The other half is having something to do instead. A blocker takes screen time away; Vread puts a book in its place.

Questions

I read on paper. Does that work?

Yes. You read on paper, an e-reader or in audio, and answer the questions in the app.

How much time a day?

20 minutes. The first book is chosen so you can finish it in 30 days at that pace.

What if I miss a day, or a week?

Your streak counts in weeks: one validated reading session in the week is enough to keep it. If you miss a whole week, a streak freeze covers it. You earn a freeze every 7 correct answers, and you can keep up to 2 in reserve.

What if a question stops me?

Use a joker: it lets you skip a question.

When does the program open?

In January 2027. You get an email on opening day with the price shown here, and you decide then.

What happens to my email address?

It is used only to tell you when the program opens. It isn’t linked to the Vread app or to any account. The details are on the privacy and terms page.
